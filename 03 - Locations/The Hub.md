# The Hub

## Short Summary

The Hub is the inhabited region that gradually forms inside AllottaNaut as Elias,
Rowen, visitors, refugees, and later generations bring pieces of their homes with them.

It should feel **accumulated rather than master-planned**.

## Origin

The first meaningful piece is Elias and Rowen's original ship: a surviving physical
fragment of [[R-HOME and Baseline|R-HOME]].

Around it appear:
- Elias's laboratories and calibration spaces,
- Rowen's attempts to make the impossible comfortable,
- storage and fabrication areas,
- guest spaces,
- structures built by rescued people,
- gardens and living systems,
- cultural and religious objects,
- archives, food, music, machines, and art from many realities.

One day the characters realize that the work camp has become a town.

Later, it may become a city or civilization.

## Core Meaning

> **AllottaNaut becomes a home made from homes.**

Some pieces may come from worlds that no longer exist. Because AllottaNaut can preserve
objects in stasis, the Hub can contain uniquely surviving fragments of otherwise lost
cultures without turning them into dead museum exhibits.

They are lived with.

## What AllottaNaut Learns

The meaning of "home" grows in stages:
1. Home is the place Elias and Rowen want to reach.
2. Home is where someone came from.
3. Home can be carried in objects, customs, language, memory, and people.
4. Home can be built.
5. A person can have more than one home.
6. Later generations may simply call AllottaNaut Home.

## Long-Term Story Function

The Hub provides a stable narrative anchor between expeditions.

It supports:
- relationships,
- research,
- politics,
- mentorship,
- daily life,
- visiting civilizations,
- divine or nonhuman guests,
- contained hazards,
- and the contrast between cosmic scale and ordinary lived experience.
