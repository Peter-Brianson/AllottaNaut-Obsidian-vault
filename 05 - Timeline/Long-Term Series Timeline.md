# Long-Term Series Timeline

## Draft Arc

1. First contact with AllottaNaut.
2. Node communication after expulsion.
3. New protagonists interact with AllottaNaut from different angles.
4. AllottaNaut experiments with game-like rules and other realities.
5. AllottaNaut learns responsibility and restraint.
6. Later cosmic-scale threats test the limits of its growth.

## August 2026 - Current Long-Form Structure

1. First contact with the newborn AllottaNaut.
2. Communication deepens; Elias and Rowen become its primary teachers.
3. The brothers retain their ship and begin serious multiversal exploration.
4. **Baseline** becomes the physics reference; **R-HOME** becomes the verified origin target.
5. Exploration evolves from direct travel to instruments, scouts, projected protection,
   and eventually avatar-like systems.
6. Pieces of visited or lost homes accumulate inside AllottaNaut and form [[The Hub]].
7. [[Refuge Civilization Overview]] makes AllottaNaut responsible for a society.
8. Eldritch containment tests Rowen's belief in the inherent value of even dangerous life.
9. [[Cursed God Overview]] teaches irreversible loss, grief, and the limits of reconstruction.
10. [[R-HOME Resolution Overview]] resolves the original home-search through a conflict
    about personhood, autonomy, humanitarian obligation, and exploitation.
11. Elias and Rowen deliberately reduce their adventuring roles for their own well-being.
12. [[Post R-HOME Era Overview]] shifts the narrative toward new protagonists.
13. AllottaNaut becomes an elder-brother presence and the Hub becomes a multiversal crossroads.
14. Later generations may be born in AllottaNaut and explore because they want to,
    not because they are lost.
