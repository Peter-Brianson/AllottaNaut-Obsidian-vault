# Future Rowen

## Short Summary

Future Rowen is the later-series version of Rowen after Game One, serving as a more relaxed, practical, emotionally grounded support figure.

## Possible Function

- Helps new characters deal with fear and confusion.
- Understands AllottaNaut through behavior and feeling more than theory.
- Provides warmth, humor, and human grounding.
- Reminds the series that wonder matters as much as danger.

## August 2026 - Post-R-HOME Direction

Future Rowen also steps back from constant adventuring after the R-HOME resolution.

His mature roles include:
- emotional anchor of the Hub,
- mentor to frightened or displaced newcomers,
- advocate for life forms others are tempted to dismiss,
- cultural bridge across radically different beings,
- source of play, humor, and companionship for AllottaNaut,
- occasional explorer when curiosity or compassion pulls him back out.

His belief that life has inherent value should be tested rather than treated as an
automatically correct answer. The eldritch containment story works because mercy only
becomes responsible once everyone else can genuinely be kept safe.
