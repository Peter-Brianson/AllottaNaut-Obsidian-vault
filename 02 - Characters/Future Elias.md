# Future Elias

## Short Summary

Future Elias is the later-series version of Elias after Game One, serving more as a teacher, support voice, researcher, or navigator than a main protagonist.

## Possible Function

- Explains AllottaNaut's rules without removing mystery.
- Warns new protagonists about overstimulation, mimicry, and node consequences.
- Helps maintain franchise continuity.
- Carries emotional weight from first contact.

## August 2026 - Post-R-HOME Direction

Future Elias is not merely an exposition voice.

He has earned the right to step back from constant crisis and gives himself time for
his own mental well-being after the R-HOME resolution.

His mature roles include:
- researcher and keeper of Baseline/R-HOME classification,
- architect of exploration doctrine and safety,
- mentor to new explorers,
- ethical skeptic who asks what rescue, intervention, and power will obligate,
- adviser on Hub policy,
- occasional expedition member when a problem truly interests or requires him.

His growth should preserve his analytical nature without reducing him to "logic."
He becomes someone who understands that precision includes moral precision.
