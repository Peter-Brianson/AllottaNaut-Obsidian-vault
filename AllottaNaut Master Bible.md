# AllottaNaut Master Bible

## Current Series Spine

The franchise begins with Elias and Rowen encountering a newborn living reality and
gradually teaching it how to interpret existence.

The first great era follows the search for [[R-HOME and Baseline|R-HOME]], the growth
of AllottaNaut from reactive awareness into moral agency, and the transformation of
the original ship/work area into [[The Hub]].

The long-form development principle is:

> **The more AllottaNaut understands reality, the more deliberately it can choose reality.**

The current major moral curriculum is:
- **Refuge civilization:** responsibility created by the ability to save.
- **Eldritch containment:** inherent value of dangerous and alien life.
- **Cursed god:** irreversible loss and identity beyond reconstruction.
- **R-HOME:** autonomy, obligation, exploitation, and whether power creates a duty to serve.

After the R-HOME resolution, Elias and Rowen intentionally reduce their frontline roles.
New protagonists inherit the exploration narrative while AllottaNaut becomes an
elder-brother figure and the setting expands into a multi-genre multiversal story engine.
