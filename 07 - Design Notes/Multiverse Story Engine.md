# Multiverse Story Engine

## Design Purpose

The multiverse should not function as a sequence of cosmetic alternate Earths.
It should allow the franchise to ask:

> **What can a universe be?**

[[R-HOME and Baseline|Baseline]] gives the audience a familiar reference frame.
Other realities can then depart from it at the level of history, biology, chemistry,
gravity, dimensions, causality, metaphysics, magic, souls, gods, or stranger rules.

## Example Reality Directions

### Matter-Dominant Cavern Reality
A universe where matter dominates over dark-energy-like expansion and gravity is not
uniform enough to create familiar galaxies. Large-scale matter forms walls, tubes,
chambers, and straw-like structures around networks of voids.

The visual inversion is important:

> Baseline has mostly emptiness with matter forming a cosmic web.
>
> This reality has mostly structured matter with emptiness forming the web.

Regional variations in physical constants could also produce "chemical climates"
where familiar compounds change stability from one region to another.

### Magic as Physics
A reality where magic is a genuine interaction rather than unexplained exception.

### Measurable Soul Systems
A reality where souls are empirically detectable, engineerable, ecological, or political.

### Causal Promise Systems
Spoken promises or names create measurable constraints.

### Dream-Matter Systems
Dreams temporarily produce physical substance.

### Machine-Spirit Systems
Sufficiently complex machines spontaneously acquire spirits.

### Horror-Law Worlds
Rules resembling horror tropes are measurable features of the reality:
isolation changes vulnerability, observation constrains entities, trauma shapes place,
or narrative coincidence has a causal mechanism.

## Tone Rule

Not every strange reality should be dangerous.

Some should be peaceful, beautiful, funny, culturally rich, or simply difficult to
understand.

## Veteran Horror

Early horror comes from ignorance:

> **We do not know what this place is.**

Later horror can come from expertise:

> **We know enough to understand how wrong this is.**

A mature AllottaNaut saying "do not leave me" should carry more weight than a loud monster.

## Character Rule

Different protagonists should care about different realities for different reasons.
The setting stays alive when exploration is driven by character interests rather than
only escalating threats.
