# R-HOME and Baseline

## Core Distinction

**R-HOME** is the specific universe Elias and Rowen originated from.

**Baseline** is the reference set of physical constants and natural behavior
measured in R-HOME.

Baseline is not a claim that their universe is metaphysically normal or privileged.
It is simply the coordinate origin of their science because every instrument they
brought with them was calibrated there.

## Relationship to Our Reality

Baseline is intended to be effectively identical, or extremely close, to the
constants and ordinary physical behavior of our real universe.

This lets the reader use familiar physics as the reference point without implying
that familiar physics is the only valid way a universe can work.

## Navigation Consequence

A Baseline match does **not** prove R-HOME.

Another reality may share:
- the same fundamental constants,
- the same chemistry,
- the same kind of stars and galaxies,
- an Earth analogue,
- a matching Solar System,
- or even astonishingly similar history.

It is still not R-HOME unless historical identity and origin are verified.

> **Baseline identifies the kind of universe. R-HOME identifies ours.**

## Philosophical Consequence

This distinction becomes one of AllottaNaut's major lessons in identity:

> Two things can be identical in observable properties and still not be the same thing.

This later connects to copies, avatars, reconstructed persons, alternate selves,
grief, and the difference between duplication and continuity.

## Scientific Language

Elias gradually stops calling familiar behavior "normal" and instead calls it
**Baseline behavior**.

That linguistic change reflects a central realization of the series:

> There may be no default way for reality to exist. Familiarity is not necessity.
