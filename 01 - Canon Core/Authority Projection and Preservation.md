# Authority, Projection, and Preservation

## Governing Rule

> **Inside AllottaNaut, AllottaNaut has authority. Outside AllottaNaut,
> the destination universe has authority.**

This is the foundational balance rule for later exploration.

AllottaNaut can alter its own interior far more naturally than it can overwrite
a foreign reality.

## Stasis Is the Native Default

Things inside AllottaNaut do not need to erode, decay, consume fuel, breathe, or
continue ordinary processes merely because Baseline physics would normally demand it.

A configuration can remain in stasis with authority until AllottaNaut chooses or
permits change.

This does not mean AllottaNaut begins with conscious mastery. Early on, it may not
understand what it is preserving or changing. The capability exists before the wisdom.

## Everything Inside Becomes Part of Its Experienced Self

Objects, organisms, structures, and processes inside AllottaNaut are not merely
tenants in empty space. They occur within AllottaNaut's body/reality and therefore
become part of what it can potentially perceive, remember, distinguish, and care about.

This supports the long-term growth of [[The Hub]].

## External Projection

AllottaNaut eventually learns to extend small regions of its chosen conditions
beyond its boundary.

Early projection may be only a few meters around an opening. Later projection can
protect a person, scout, vehicle, or larger expedition.

A projected region can carry selected Baseline-like conditions such as:
- breathable atmosphere,
- pressure,
- temperature range,
- compatible electromagnetism,
- stable chemistry,
- gravity behavior,
- or deeper constants required to keep Baseline matter viable.

## The Real Limiter

AllottaNaut is not primarily limited by a conventional energy bar.

It is limited by:
- comprehension,
- attention,
- precision,
- knowledge of what must be preserved,
- knowledge of the destination's rules,
- and the complexity of the boundary between two rule systems.

> **The more AllottaNaut understands reality, the more deliberately it can choose reality.**

## Preservation Is Not the Same as Protection

AllottaNaut can preserve something inside itself without automatically knowing how
to protect that same thing outside itself.

To carry a person safely into a hostile reality, it must understand the person's
required conditions well enough to carry those conditions with them.

This prevents later power from becoming arbitrary deus ex machina.
