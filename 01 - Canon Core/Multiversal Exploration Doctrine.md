# Multiversal Exploration Doctrine

## Why Exploration Changes

The first foreign realities are approached with too little information.
Elias and Rowen may physically take their ship through an opening, retreat when
possible, and learn the hard way that Baseline technology cannot be assumed to
function under foreign laws.

Exploration therefore becomes a discipline.

## Practical Progression

A likely mature doctrine is:

1. **AllottaNaut senses what it can from the boundary.**
2. **Instrument package** crosses first.
3. **Disposable or recoverable automated scout** follows.
4. **Protected vehicle or ship** enters if the environment is sufficiently understood.
5. **People** enter only after survival assumptions are tested.

The exact order can change when a reality breaks the assumptions behind a step.

## The Original Ship

Elias and Rowen retain their original vessel.

It becomes:
- laboratory,
- archive,
- Baseline calibration reference,
- fabrication platform,
- transport,
- and the oldest surviving physical piece of R-HOME inside AllottaNaut.

As [[The Hub]] grows, the ship remains emotionally important even when more capable
vessels can be built.

## Automated Scouts

Scouts should be modular and expendable because a foreign reality may:
- destroy ordinary matter,
- make electronics impossible,
- alter chemistry,
- change how gravity behaves,
- invalidate familiar sensors,
- or return telemetry that Baseline mathematics cannot initially interpret.

## AllottaNaut's External Senses

Watching the brothers build machines to experience environments they cannot survive
teaches AllottaNaut a new idea:

> **I can put part of myself somewhere else.**

AllottaNaut may eventually form external sensory extensions or proxy bodies rather
than relying only on machines.

## Avatar Stages

Later exploration may develop through three conceptually different systems:

### Remote Avatar
A body is controlled by a person whose original body remains safely inside AllottaNaut.
This is advanced telepresence.

### Copied Person
AllottaNaut reproduces a person's physical/neural state in another body.
The copy awakens believing it is the person, creating a genuine identity problem.

### Continuous Extension
AllottaNaut attempts to maintain one continuous conscious process distributed between
the original person and an external body.

Whether this is truly continuity rather than hidden duplication should remain a major
philosophical and scientific question.

## Core Design Rule

Safer exploration should increase the range of stories the cast can enter without
eliminating danger. Unknown rules, incomplete models, attention limits, bad assumptions,
and moral uncertainty must remain meaningful.
