# Cursed God Arc

## Premise

The cast encounters a god in a reality that has become a barren hellscape.

The god's pantheon has been cleansed or erased from the reality. Its ability to
communicate effectively has been crippled by a curse or equivalent metaphysical injury.

It remains conscious and enduring, but cannot recover those it loved and cannot even
end its own existence.

## Central Horror

The arc is not primarily about defeating a god or curse.

It is about discovering a form of loss that even extraordinary power cannot undo.

AllottaNaut may be able to:
- search for traces,
- preserve memories,
- reconstruct forms,
- reproduce voices,
- or create extremely accurate copies.

None of those acts necessarily restore the historical identity of the people who were lost.

## Philosophical Link

This directly develops the lesson in [[R-HOME and Baseline]]:

> **Identical properties do not guarantee identical identity.**

A perfect reconstruction may still be a new being.

## AllottaNaut's Lesson

AllottaNaut, whose native interior can preserve things indefinitely, must learn that
absence and loss are not the same.

> **Something existed, mattered, and cannot be recovered as that same something.**

This becomes one of AllottaNaut's first genuine encounters with irreversible grief.

## Theme

> **What does loss mean when power is no longer enough?**

The story should remain morally serious and resist an easy reversal that restores the
pantheon at the end.
