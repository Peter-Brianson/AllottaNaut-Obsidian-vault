# Strange Universe Arc

## Short Summary

A future arc where characters become protected nodes of AllottaNaut while traveling through strange universes and hostile planets.

## Rule Hook

AllottaNaut can protect characters from some reality-altering effects, such as not needing to breathe, but cannot prevent direct physical harm like crushing, slicing, burning, or impact.

## Inspiration Direction

Rain World, Scavengers Reign, Samurai Jack, The Midnight Gospel, Fantastic Planet, and abstract geometry worlds.

## August 2026 - Expanded Direction

The earlier fixed protection rule is superseded by the authority/projection model in
[[Authority Projection and Preservation]].

The strongest direction for this arc is not merely hostile planets. It is exposure to
**different answers to what a universe can be**.

Priority examples include:
- matter-dominant Swiss-cheese/cavern cosmologies,
- non-uniform or regional gravity,
- chemistry that changes from region to region,
- weak- or strong-gravity universes,
- realities with magic as a fundamental interaction,
- realities with measurable souls or gods,
- and realities whose causal structure resembles horror logic.

Baseline should never be described as objectively normal. It is only the reference
physics Elias and Rowen happened to be born into.

See [[R-HOME and Baseline]] and [[Multiverse Story Engine]].
